What is a shift? A shift tracks the cash and payments a cashier is responsible for, from the moment they start (with a starting cash float) to the moment they close and count what's actually in the drawer. Every sale is tagged to the shift so the system knows what total it expects — closing compares that to what was physically counted, and flags any difference (variance) for the admin to review.
